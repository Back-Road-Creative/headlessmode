PMBOK ITTO reference

Theory reference — the 49 predictive processes on the knowledge-area × process-group grid, independent of any project's state. Follow a cell for its inputs, tools & techniques and outputs.

How to read this page

Four words carry this page, and none of them explains itself:

ITTO
What each process page lists: its inputs, tools & techniques and outputs — what you need, how you do it, and what you get.
Predictive
Planned up front against one approved baseline, rather than re-decided each sprint. These 49 are the predictive set; a project running agile or hybrid reads most of its controls against the team's own cadence instead.
Knowledge area
A knowledge area is one of the ten subject groupings PMBOK sorts project work into, like cost or risk. It is one row of the grid below.
Process group
A process group is one of the five logical groupings, like planning or closing, every PMBOK process belongs to. It is one column of the grid below.
