Critical chain (Driftless extension)

Not a PMBOK-6 clause — no numbered process names this technique (tt.EXTENSIONS). The project buffer is sized at half the safety recorded on the critical chain's own three-point estimates; a feeding buffer, also half its task's own safety, sits wherever a non-critical task merges directly into the chain. A task with no three-point estimate on record contributes zero safety here — never an invented figure.
