The method map

This map shows every process, technique and artifact this product knows, and how they connect to each other.

How to read this map

Every process, technique and artifact this catalog knows, with every tie between them drawn as a line. Three kinds, one shape each: ◻ a process — a step the method names, drawn as a filled square, tinted by its knowledge area; ● a technique — a way of doing one, drawn as a filled dot; ◆ an artifact — a document or record a process reads or produces, drawn as a filled diamond. Every shape opens its own summary below the map, on click or on Enter, and its own name shows on hover and on keyboard focus while its ties light up with it. Every shape still links onward too: its own process, technique or artifact page is in this export as well. A shape's summary and its ties show while you hover it or tab to it; press Space on a shape you have tabbed to to keep them up.

How to read this page

Shapes are process, technique or artifact; fill colour is knowledge area or technique family; a dashed line is optional. The legend below spells out every shape, fill and line. Filters and search narrow what is drawn; Fit/100%/+/− zoom the picture; a shape's own summary and ties show on hover, focus, or Space.

Shown without any project's reading: this export draws the method in theory. Washing these processes with one project's own progress is a read of the live product, not a page in this bundle.
