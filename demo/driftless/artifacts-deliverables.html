Deliverables

Deliverables artifact · ← Artifact catalog

What it is

The actual things the project is building or producing.

Why it matters

It is the point of the whole exercise — everything else exists to help produce these.

What it looks like here

Not stored separately: read directly off each task's status and how much of it is complete.
