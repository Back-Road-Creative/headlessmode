Requirements Documentation

Documents artifact · ← Artifact catalog

What it is

A written record of what people actually need the project to deliver.

Why it matters

Without it, 'what was asked for' becomes a matter of memory and opinion.

What it looks like here

Stored as a short piece of prose attached to the project.
