Technique library

Every technique Driftless works with, explained — the tools and techniques the PMBOK ITTO catalog names, and any Driftless adds to its own vocabulary that no process names — when to use each, when not to, the steps, what it produces and what usually goes wrong. Theory reference: nothing here reads a project or a date.

How to read this page

The library is grouped by family — the kind of work a technique belongs to. Each family opens with what it is for, then one row per technique: its name, what it is for in a line, and how much help this product gives you with it. Open any technique for when to use it, when not to, the steps in order, what it produces and what usually goes wrong.

Runnable here
Driftless has an assistant you can open and run for it — 55 of 90.
Worksheet
No calculator, but a printable worksheet walks you through it — 35 of 90.
Anything else
The column holds the one sentence recorded beside that technique saying why this product explains it rather than running it — 0 of 90.
