Agreements Initial

Environment artifact · ← Artifact catalog

What it is

The contract, letter of intent or other agreement with the customer or sponsor that the project starts from.

Why it matters

It records what the customer or sponsor initially intends, and is often the basis on which the project is started.

What it looks like here

Not tracked: pre-project agreements happen elsewhere — once one is signed, read it as an agreement.

Made by

No process in the catalog produces this artifact.

Used by

  • 4.1 Develop Project Charter

This product does not track this artifact against a real project's data — pre-project Agreements live elsewhere — signed ones are Agreements.

Reached from a project's own process map, this page also shows what that project's own data says about this artifact.
