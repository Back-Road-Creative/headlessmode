Project Charter

Plans artifact · ← Artifact catalog

What it is

A short paper that officially starts the project and says who is in charge.

Why it matters

Without it nobody has the standing to spend money or ask people for time — it is the permission slip the rest of the project depends on.

What it looks like here

Driftless generates it on demand from the project's own record (its sponsor, goal and manager), rather than storing a separate copy that could drift from them.
