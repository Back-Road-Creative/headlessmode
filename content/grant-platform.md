[Open the fixed, read-only demo](/demo/grant-platform/). It is generated from the platform's
demo scenario as of 2026-09-01: three organizations, a catalog of opportunities,
applications, awards, engagements, tasks and documents, shown on the product's own pages
with the write controls removed. It cannot save changes.

Grant Platform keeps grant work in one database: finding opportunities, preparing
applications, administering awards and tracking the reports an award requires. It replaces
two earlier systems, one for discovery and applications and one for award administration, so
a grant does not have to be re-entered when an application becomes an award.

## What it covers

- **Discovery.** A catalog of opportunities that can be matched against an organization's
  recorded focus areas and geography. Matches are ranked by how many of those signals
  agree, then by close date. A closed opportunity is not a match, and an organization with
  no recorded geography is treated as unknown rather than as everywhere.
- **Applications.** Shortlisting an opportunity creates an application with its checklist.
  Required items must be done before submission, and application documents are drafted from
  details already on file.
- **Awards.** An award moves through seven stages, from pre-award to post-closeout.
  Confirming its terms generates a reporting schedule that covers the period of performance.
- **Reporting.** Each reporting obligation has a due date and a status. An award cannot enter
  closeout while an obligation is still open. Reports include an activity log, an artifact
  register, a work schedule and opportunity matches.

The platform tracks what an award requires and when it is due. Meeting a funder's
requirements is still up to the people doing the work.

## Who manages it

Staff roles (grant manager, specialist, finance, consultant, administrator) run the work.
Funder and recipient members of an organization see that organization's records. An install
can turn on the recipient side, the funder side or both.

## Example workflow

A recipient shortlists an opportunity that matches its programs. The application's checklist
is worked through and the application is submitted. When it is awarded, a grant manager
records the award's terms and period of performance, and the platform generates the reporting
calendar. Each report is marked submitted as it goes out. When the last obligation is closed
and the closeout checklist is submitted, the award can be closed out.

## Status and access
