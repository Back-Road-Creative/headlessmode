I'm JP, and I run Headless Mode, a one-person software studio in Franklin, North Carolina. I build software for music practice, project and grant work, media production, and exploring rural places. The [home page](/#products) lists the products; each has its own page describing what it does and what access it has.

## Studio and practice

My consulting practice is [Back Road Creative](https://backroadcreative.com). Headless Mode publishes the software; Back Road Creative scopes and runs the engagements that adapt these products to delivery work.

To ask about a product or a project, email hello@headlessmode.com or see [Contact](/contact/). Code and issues are on [GitHub](https://github.com/Back-Road-Creative); shorter updates are on [X](https://x.com/HeadlessMode).
